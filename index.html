About Us

About Swami Consultancy

Swami Consultancy is a trusted career and placement partner for students and fresh graduates across India. We work closely with every candidate to understand their strengths, ambitions and readiness for the job market.

Our team provides end-to-end career guidance, from building an ATS-friendly resume to preparing for interviews with confidence. Through our growing network of hiring partners, we connect candidates with opportunities that genuinely match their skills.

With a focus on personalised support and professional development, we don't just help you find a job — we help you build a career.
